## 1. Lawyer cost — sharper than "$500"

## 2. "The math doesn't work" — claim-size data

No published UZ median, but legal architecture maps the distribution:

- **Simplified civil procedure** triggers at claim **≤ 5 BHK = 2.06M sum (~$170)** for physical persons ([UNHCR rimap](https://rimap.unhcr.org/node/62750); BHK = 412K sum, [WageIndicator Aug 2025](https://wageindicator.org/salary/minimum-wage/minimum-wages-news/2025/minimum-wage-updated-in-uzbekistan-from-01-august-2025-august-03-2025)). The legislature built a "small-claims" track *because* most personal claims fall under this.
- **Simplified debt-collection (court order)** **expanded Apr 2026** to all claims **≤ 5 BHK (~$170)** — utilities, HOA, postal ([Zamon.uz](https://zamon.uz/en/2026/04/23/the-procedure-for-collecting-debts-for-utility-services-in-uzbekistan-has-been-simplified)).
- **Water-utility arrears**: **148,000 cases→court in 2025 totaling 392B sum** = mean **~2.65M sum / ~$220 per case** ([Kun.uz, 12 Jan 2026](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/01/12/household-water-debts-in-uzbekistan-fall-by-nearly-half-in-2025)).
- **Min wage** 1.27M sum/mo (~$105); **avg wage** 6.38M sum (~$530), 2025. Typical unpaid-salary claim = 1–3 mo wages = **~$100–$1,600** (commonly $300–$1,200 **[ESTIMATE]**).
- **Alimony floor:** 306K sum/mo — *less than half* the 669K min consumer budget ([Zamin.uz, 27 Jun 2025](https://zamin.uz/en/uzbekistan/153236-hundreds-of-thousands-of-children-await-justice-will-the-alimony-system-be-reviewed.html)).

**Pitch line:** *"Law itself sorts most citizen claims under ~$170. Avg utility-debt case is ~$220. Avg unpaid-salary claim is ~$100–$1,200. Lawyer to file it: **$500**. Economics are upside-down — you lose either way."*

## 3. Top 6–8 civil-dispute scenarios — prioritized

1. **Utility-debt cases** — ~$170–$415 claim. **148K water cases→courts in 2025**, 462.9B sum owed ([Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/01/12/household-water-debts-in-uzbekistan-fall-by-nearly-half-in-2025)); ~200K utility/yr per Aug 2025 decree.
2. **Developer/застройщик fraud** — $4K–$40K claim. **Eco Dream / Glamour Gold: ~300 victims, 97B sum** ([Vzglyad May 2026](https://vzglyad.uz/2026/05/01/v-tashkente-zastrojshhik-obmanul-sotni-zhitelej-pochti-na-100-mlrd-sumov/)); **1,300+ housing-fraud cases 2023, 1,700+ complaints H1 2024** ([news.mail.ru](https://news.mail.ru/economics/64188865/)); Samarkand developer got 9.5 yrs ([news.mail.ru](https://news.mail.ru/incident/65243188/)); escrow decree 2025 ([nuz.uz](https://nuz.uz/2025/02/13/v-uzbekistane-vvodyatsya-novye-mery-protiv-zloupotreblenij-zastrojshhikov/)).
3. **E-commerce / defective goods** — $17–$415. **~3,100 platform complaints 2025**: Uzum Market 851 (27.8%), Uzum Nasiya 842 (27.6%), Yandex 329; **9.6M consumers helped, 7.3B sum recovered** ([Kun.uz 12 Feb 2026](https://kun.uz/en/news/2026/02/12/competition-committee-secures-uzs-73-billion-for-consumers-in-online-shopping-disputes)).
4. **Unpaid salary / labor** — $100–$1,600. **2M+ civil cases 2025, +50% YoY** ([gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/en/2026/02/11/courts/)); flagship demo.
5. **Alimony / child support** — 306K+ sum/mo (~$25/mo). **46,900 divorces 2025** ([Daryo](https://daryo.uz/en/2026/04/28/uzbekistan-records-46900-divorces-as-rate-remains-below-regional-levels)); "hundreds of thousands of children await alimony" — Senate Jun 2025.
6. **Microloan / personal debt** — microloan cap **doubled to 100M sum** in 2025 ([UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/en/the-central-bank-of-uzbekistan-approves-new-macroprudential-standards-for-banks-and-microfinance-institutions/)); 3-yr limitations; Civil Code-governed.
7. **Traffic / OSAGO excess** — $400–$6,600+. OSAGO cap raised to 80M sum; excess→civil court ([EAI.uz](https://eai.uz/en/articles/osago-fines-uzbekistan-2025)).
8. **Neighbor / noise** — small admin fine + civil moral-damage. Nighttime-noise penalty Sep 2025, civil-court remedy explicit ([one.uz](https://one.uz/en/news/uzbekistan/20593-penalty-and-liability-established-for-nighttime-noise.html)).

Top 4 (utility / dev fraud / consumer / wage) = demo-day priority. **Developer fraud is *the* visceral hook** for Andijan.

## 4. Legaltech pricing benchmarks — $5–8 B2C and $30+ B2B both check out

**Consumer (2025–26):** **DoNotPay $18/mo** ([aihungry](https://aihungry.com/tools/donotpay/pricing)); **Rocket Lawyer $39.99/mo or $39.99/doc** ([rocketlawyer.com/pricing](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/pricing)); **LegalZoom $59/doc**, LLC $0+state, DBA $99+state ([law-trust.com](https://law-trust.com/blog/legalzoom-pricing-guide-2026)); **LegalShield $24.95–$59.95/mo prepaid** ([legalshield.com/pricing](https://www.legalshield.com/pricing)); **Pravoved.ru (RU)** freemium + paid lawyer, **6.5M visits/mo, 20K lawyers** ([Law.com](https://www.law.com/legaltechnews/2018/02/13/russias-pravoved-takes-on-robot-lawyer-for-free-legal-services/)).

**B2B law-firm SaaS (per lawyer/mo, annual):** **Clio Manage $49 → $159** with Clio Duo AI ([clio.com/pricing](https://www.clio.com/pricing/)); **PracticePanther $49 → $114** ([practicepanther.com/pricing](https://www.practicepanther.com/pricing/)).

**Verdict:** Areeza's **$4–8/filing** B2C is **2–10× cheaper than DoNotPay's monthly fee** and **5–7× cheaper than a single Rocket/LegalZoom document** — right wedge for a $530-avg-wage market. **B2B $30/filing reads LOW** vs $49+/lawyer-mo unbundled. Consider **$30/filing + $99/firm/mo** — still 1/3 of PracticePanther; fits "infrastructure for the 5,665 Uzbek lawyers" framing.
