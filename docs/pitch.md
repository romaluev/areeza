## Slide 3 — The problem (Muammo) — both sides of the market

- **Citizen side:** courts are online (e-sud), but people don't know which claim, which form, which code — so filings get **rejected for procedural mistakes**, and a lawyer costs **$125–300+** against a **$100/mo** minimum wage. Most give up.
- **Court side:** **2M+ civil cases/yr (+50% YoY)**; judges carry **556 cases/month vs. a 16/month norm** — the Supreme Court itself calls it "an unrealistic regime."
- 🎤 *"Two problems, one root cause: the citizen can't translate their life problem into the right procedure — so they're shut out, and the courts drown in messy filings. We fix both."* (**B1 relevance, S5 impact.**)

## Slide 4 — Why now (Nega aynan hozir)

1. **Filing just became electronic-only** (Presidential Decree, 21 Aug 2025).
2. **The state is putting AI inside the courts** — an AI assistant on my.sud.uz, outcome prediction, auto-drafting → **government endorsement + a built-in buyer.**
3. **LLMs crossed the threshold** to read a messy story and produce legal language — but raw LLMs hallucinate; the unlock is *constrained, validated* generation against real forms.
4. **National mandate:** "Digital Uzbekistan 2030" + "Zero Bureaucracy," 16M+ digital services in H1 2025.
- 🎤 *"This was impossible 18 months ago. The rails went live, the AI mandate landed, and the models got good — all at once. We're early, on purpose."*
